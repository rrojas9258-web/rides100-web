Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Rides100, operated by RISCASAN LLC, collects information needed to provide ridesharing services, including account information, trip information, payment-related information, device information, and location information when required for ride and driver features.

We use information to operate the service, process rides and payments, support safety and compliance, prevent fraud and abuse, provide customer support, and improve the product.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers. Payment card details may be processed by payment providers and are not intended to be stored directly by Rides100.

Location data is used when required for pickup, routing, active trips and driver availability. Users may control device permissions through their device settings, although some features may not function without required permissions.

For privacy questions or requests, contact contact@riscasan.com.
