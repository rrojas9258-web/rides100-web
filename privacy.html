PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Rides100 is operated by RISCASAN LLC.

Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, Rides100 may collect account and contact information, trip and ride-request information, device and app information, approximate or precise location when permission is granted and needed for service features, driver and vehicle information, support communications, and payment-related transaction information.

How information is used

Information may be used to operate rider and driver features, support trip matching and routing, process or reconcile payments, provide customer support, maintain safety and compliance processes, detect fraud or abuse, protect accounts, troubleshoot the service, and improve product reliability.

Location information

Location information may be needed for pickup, destination, driver availability, navigation, active-trip features, and safety or support functions. Device permissions can be changed through your operating-system settings, although some Rides100 features may not work without required location access.

Payments

Payment services may be provided by third-party payment processors. Rides100 does not intend to store full payment-card numbers in its own application databases. Payment providers may process information under their own privacy practices.

Sharing and service providers

Rides100 may share information with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the platform, including infrastructure, mapping, communications, payment, safety, identity, compliance, and support services. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a legitimate corporate transaction.

Data protection

Rides100 uses technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so users should protect their account credentials and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

Data choices and requests

Users may control certain device permissions through their device settings. Privacy-related access, correction, deletion, or other requests can be sent to contact@riscasan.com. Availability of specific rights may depend on applicable law and the status of the Rides100 service in the user's market.

Launch status

Rides100 is still preparing for public launch. This policy describes the intended privacy framework for the service and may be updated as features, markets, vendors, and legal requirements are finalized.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact contact@riscasan.com.
