FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the Rides100 launch experience.

Is Rides100 live yet?

Rides100 is preparing for launch. Public download links will be posted when the app is ready and launch requirements are complete.

Can riders pay cash?

No. Ride fares are designed to be paid in the app. Tips are optional.

How do riders choose a driver?

The rider experience is designed to let riders review available driver offers and choose an option before the trip.

How do drivers get paid?

Driver payouts are planned through the platform payment system, with earnings and payout status shown in the driver experience.

Who operates Rides100?

Rides100 is operated by RISCASAN LLC.

How do I contact support?

Use the Support page or email contact@riscasan.com.

Where will Rides100 launch?

Launch markets will be announced as regulatory, insurance, operational, and app-readiness requirements are completed.

Can I sign up as a driver now?

Driver interest is welcome, but activation depends on market availability and completion of applicable onboarding requirements.
