RIDERS

Your ride. Your choice.

Rides100 is preparing an app experience where riders can request transportation, review driver offers, choose an option, and pay in the app.

1. Request

Enter pickup and destination and submit a ride request.

2. Review offers

Compare available driver offers and choose the one that works for you.

3. Ride & pay

Complete the trip with in-app payment. No cash fares. Tips are optional.

App availability

Rides100 is currently preparing its launch experience. App Store and Google Play links will be posted here when public downloads are available.
